Salmonella Control Options Finder

Frozen raw breaded chicken products. Answer a few plain-language questions to find out whether the CFIA Salmonella controls apply to your product and which control option fits your process.

Plain-language glossary

Log reduction (for example, "7-log")
A way to describe how many bacteria are killed. Each 1-log reduction kills 90% of them. A 7-log reduction cuts Salmonella by a factor of 10 million.
Lethality process
A heat (or other kill) step that has been shown to reduce bacteria by a set number of logs.
Validated
Backed by evidence, such as a scientific study or in-plant testing, that the process really achieves the reduction every time it is run correctly.
Par-fried
Partly fried, usually to set the coating, while the chicken inside is still raw.
Non-intact
Ground, chopped or formed chicken. This includes products formed from several whole muscle pieces, and chicken mixed with other ingredients.
Lot
The group of product treated as one unit for sampling. What counts as a lot depends on the option.
Sample unit
One separate portion of product sent to the lab for testing.
Presumptive positive
A screening test result that suggests Salmonella is present but has not yet been confirmed by a second test.

About this tool

This tool follows the CFIA page Salmonella control options in frozen raw breaded chicken products (page last modified 2019-04-01). That page carried a reminder that the measures had to be in place by April 1, 2019. Always check the CFIA page for the latest wording, since requirements can change.

The tool is a learning and planning aid. It does not replace your preventive control plan, advice from a qualified food safety professional, or a decision by CFIA. If your product does not fit neatly into a question, ask CFIA.
